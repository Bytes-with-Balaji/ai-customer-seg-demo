The shift

From one-size-fits-all to one-customer-at-a-time

This is the entire idea of the next two hours, in one picture.

Traditional approach

All customers→ Same campaign→ Same message→ Same treatment

Efficient to run. Expensive in a different way — it wastes attention on customers who didn't need it, and misses the ones who did.

AI-enabled approach

Customer data→ Understand→ Segment→ Predict behaviour→ Personalize action

Same customer base. Different question asked of the data at every step: who is this customer, right now?

The business flow we'll build, end to end

Customer Data→Segmentation→ Behaviour→Churn / Retention Risk→ Reason→Next Best Action→ Retain / Cross-sell / Upsell→Business Outcome

Growth, responsibly

Cross-sell and upsell — the difference, live

Cross-sell = a different relevant product. Upsell = more value within the product they already have.

CROSS-SELL EXAMPLE

Customer with 5-year motor policy

Good payment history · High engagement · Stable relationship · No major complaints

AI-identified opportunity: Health insurance

Reason: customer profile and existing relationship indicate potential suitability — not a guarantee of fit.

Suggested action: Offer a personalized health-insurance conversation.

AI identifies the opportunity. The customer must still be offered any product based on suitability and consent.

UPSELL EXAMPLE

Customer with basic motor coverage

High vehicle usage · High-value vehicle · Strong engagement · Coverage gap identified

AI-identified opportunity: Enhanced coverage / relevant add-ons

Suggested action: Explore whether enhanced coverage is appropriate for this vehicle profile.

Same product family, more appropriate value — this is upsell, not cross-sell.

The most important idea today

Don't always sell

HIGH CHURN RISK · RECENT COMPLAINT · POOR CLAIMS EXPERIENCE

A customer has a recent complaint, a negative sentiment signal, and a poor claims experience. Renewal is approaching.

What should the insurer do next?

❌ Do not cross-sell   ❌ Do not upsell   ✅ Service recovery first

Customer-centric AI is not about maximizing sales on every interaction. Selling into an unresolved problem accelerates the very churn you're trying to prevent.

Governance

What if the model performs worse for one customer group?

What should the insurer do?

The strongest answer combines several of these: investigate the data, validate performance across groups, review for bias, and keep human oversight in place — "continue using it unchanged" is never the answer on its own.

Responsible AI in this context rests on: Accuracy · Fairness · Explainability · Privacy · Consent · Governance · Human oversight.

Closing

Customer retention isn't about stopping customers from leaving.

It's about understanding customers early enough to create a better reason for them to stay.

WHO→WHAT→WHY→WHAT NEXT

Segmentation tells us who the customer is. Predictive AI tells us what they may do. Explainable AI helps us understand why. Next Best Action tells us what to do. Customer experience decides whether they stay.
